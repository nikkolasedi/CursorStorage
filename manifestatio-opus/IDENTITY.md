# @Manifestatio.Opus visual identity

Locked to the Michelin carousel in `week-2026-09-28/01-michelin/slides/`. Match that paper, light, and marks. Do not invent a new template.

## Positioning

**The Duolingo for business enthusiasts.** Famous-company mechanisms, rewritten as handmade research notes.

- Mission: one stealable mechanism per post, crafted like someone sat with a pen for hours.
- Values: craft over polish, mechanism over hype, stealable lessons.
- Voice: specific, calm, slightly obsessive. Numbers over adjectives.

### Caption do's

- Lead with the twist. Make it swipeable and specific.
- Name the company or the mechanism in the first two lines.
- One stealable lesson. Sign `@Manifestatio.Opus`.
- Instagram auto-post: interesting caption, still no guru speak. 4–8 short lines is fine.

### Caption don'ts

- Guru speak, "unleash", hashtag walls.
- Fake diary intimacy.
- Emojis as decoration.
- Invented statistics.
- Claims the brief forbids.

## Paper and photography

- Cream, slightly textured sheet with **faint grid lines**.
- Dark walnut desk, sheet edges visible, slight curl and imperfections.
- Soft natural window light from **top left**. Gentle paper shadows.
- Overhead, slight angle. Photographed artifact, not a digital mockup.
- Tactile extras: one paper clip **or** one piece of tape **or** one sticky note per slide. Never all three.
- Crop: **1080x1350** (4:5). Generate 3:4 then crop if needed.

## Typography

- Body: classic typewriter serif. Exact spelling from the brief.
- Margin notes, arrows, `@Manifestatio.Opus`, slide number: messy black ballpoint.
- Slide number `N/8` top right. Handle `@Manifestatio.Opus` bottom, handwritten.

## Marker system

| Color | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Yellow | `#F2D46B` | key insight |
| Red | `#C44536` | warning / emphasis |
| Blue | `#3B7EA1` | numbers / action |
| Green | `#7A9E5C` | money / growth |

Marks are slightly imperfect and overlap the text. Mark **1–2 phrases only** per slide.

## Photo print rule

Every slide, including slide 8, gets a **realistic glossy photo print** of the actual product, place, or artifact from the story:

- White border, taped or clipped at a slight angle, soft shadow.
- Cover (slide 1): print takes about **40%** of the sheet.
- Slides 2–8: smaller print is fine, still clearly a photo, not an illustration.
- No faces. No extra logos drawn on the paper. Product branding *inside* the taped photo is allowed.

## Layout

Always **8 slides**.

1. Cover:
   - Stories 1–3: photo + hook.
   - **Story 4 onward:** quiz cover. Locked. See **Quiz cover (from story 4)** below.
2–6. Mechanism, numbers, plot. Reveal quiz answers in the story, not as a scorecard.
7. Lesson. More whitespace. One handwritten star.
8. **Always** this exact closer:

> This is one story on Manifestatio.Opus.
> The Duolingo for business enthusiasts.
> Try it now.
> Launch your next business idea.

Yellow highlight on **Duolingo for business enthusiasts**. Blue arrow / underline on the last line. Handwritten `link in bio`.

Body text: about **8–18 words** per slide besides the closer. No extra copy.

## Quiz cover (from story 4)

Locked. Every carousel from story 4 onward (Samwer, then Hyrox, Crocs, Dyson, and later weeks) opens on a quiz. Stories 1–3 keep the hook cover.

- Same paper identity as every other slide: cream grid, walnut desk, typewriter body, handwritten `@Manifestatio.Opus`, `1/8`, photo print ~40%.
- 1–3 questions. Mix multiple choice and fill-in-the-blank. Two is the default so the photo stays large.
- Questions are about a surprising fact that the later slides actually answer. Do **not** mark the correct answers on slide 1.
- No question may contain another question's answer. If Q1 names the company or the mechanism, Q2 cannot ask that same fact.
- Proofread every proper noun against the brief. Lookalike names (Alando vs Zalando) must not share the quiz cover. Use the name the question needs; keep the other name for later slides.
- Handwritten lock-in, exact line: `If you can't answer this, you have to read this.` Small swipe arrow is fine. Do not add extra slogans.
- Reveal answers in the story slides, in the mechanism, not as a scorecard.

Locked Samwer example: `week-2026-09-28/04-samwer/quiz-cover-example/01-quiz-example.jpg`.

Cream grid on walnut + `@Manifestatio.Opus` + `N/8` + one tactile object + one product photo print.
