What if the fastest way to a billion-dollar company wasn't to invent, but to copy?

The Samwer brothers cloned eBay as Alando and sold it back in 100 days for about $43 million. Then they ran the same play again: CityDeal sold back to Groupon for about $126 million in stock, and their Zappos-style Zalando IPO'd around €5.3 billion.

Their edge wasn't creativity. It was speed, and proof that execution often beats invention.
