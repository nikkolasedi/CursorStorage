## User story (keep the mechanism)

McDonald's doesn't make most of its money from burgers. It makes it from real estate. Franchisees don't just pay for branding. They rent the land from McDonald's itself. The company buys prime locations, then leases them back to operators. Even if a franchise struggles, McDonald's still gets paid. Rent, fees, control. One of the largest real estate empires in the world, disguised as a burger chain.

- Franchise/license agreements **generally have 20-year terms**. At the end, McDonald's keeps the dirt and can re-franchise or close.
- ~95% of restaurants were franchised at year-end 2024 (context; not required on a slide).
