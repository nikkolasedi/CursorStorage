McDonald's doesn't make most of its money from burgers.

Harry Sonneborn's line: they are not in the hamburger business. They are in real estate. Franchisees rent the land, on leases that generally run 20 years.

2024: $10.0 billion in rent vs $5.6 billion in royalties. About 56% of the land. About 80% of the buildings. Even if a store struggles, the landlord still gets paid.

The burger is the tenant. The land is the business.
