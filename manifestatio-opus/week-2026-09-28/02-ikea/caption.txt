IKEA doesn't want you to find what you're looking for, at least not quickly. Ingvar Kamprad designed every store as a one-way labyrinth, so a visit stretches to about two hours and roughly half of what you buy was never on the list. The meatballs are not a side dish; they are dwell time, the best sofa-seller in the building. Don't just design the product: design the path to the till.
