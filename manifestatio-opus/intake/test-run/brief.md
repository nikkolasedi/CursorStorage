## Story (Manifestatio retelling)

A teenager walks from the Jura to Paris, learns packing from the inside, and spots one practical flaw in luggage: rounded tops don't stack. His flat, canvas-covered trunk fits the railway and steamship era. Copycats follow, so the family turns the name itself into the pattern. The famous monogram started as an anti-counterfeit device.
